How BidRoom works

Three roles in one contract

RoleWhoWhat they do
ClientCreates the projectLocks the budget, writes criteria and milestones, reviews each delivery.
BidderAny other walletLocks a bond, posts a price, a time and a proposal. Can withdraw until bidding closes.
WinnerThe selected bidderConfirms the award, delivers one link per milestone, gets paid as work is approved.
AnyoneAny walletTriggers the steps nobody can block: selection, rulings, auto-approval, refunds.

The four phases

Bidding runs for the window the client chose. Selection starts when it closes: anyone can trigger the blind AI evaluation. Bidders are labelled B0, B1 and so on, the evaluator never sees an address, and it may use only the written criteria. Every score and the reasoning are stored on-chain. If the best weighted score is below 34% of the maximum, nobody is awarded and everybody is refunded.

Delivery pays milestone by milestone. The client approves, or is silent: silence for the whole review window counts as approval. Dispute lets the client contest a delivery inside the review window; an AI arbiter fetches the delivered page and judges it against the acceptance text written before bidding, and must quote the page to be valid.

What to know

  • Use links that cannot change after delivery (for example a GitHub file at a commit). The arbiter reads the page when it rules.
  • The arbiter and the selector are language models. The contract limits them: they choose among fixed outcomes, must quote evidence, and every validator re-derives the decision independently.
  • If a ruling or a selection cannot be reached, nothing is recorded and anyone can try again. Unresolved disputes split the milestone 50/50 after 72 hours.
  • Payouts are credited first and withdrawn by their owner, so no one else can block your money.
